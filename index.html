👤 About Me

Yasho Krishna Priya is a passionate Bharatanatyam dancer with 10 years of training under the guidance of Parshwanath Upadhye, Shruti Gopal, and Aditya PV at Upadhye School of Dance. She has performed at the Temple of Fine Arts, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, and various venues in Bangalore and across Karnataka. She has been honored with the prestigious Natya Kala Ilavarasi award in 2019 for her excellence in dance. She completed the SĀDHAKA Level 1 course with Distinction at Sridevi Nrithyalaya and the Karnataka State Dr. Gangubai Hanagal Junior Exam with Distinction in 2024. She is also a certified yoga practitioner with 8 years of experience, winner of the CISCE Yoga Competition in 2024, and the Junior Miss India 2026 titleholder, winning Miss Creative and Miss Talented awards. Her dedication to the arts and wellness, combined with her grace and poise, has made her a role model for young women.
